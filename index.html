How the studio runs
BRIEF → SYSTEM → LAUNCH → SCALE

Our process is built on clear steps, but it only works because of the people who execute them. My role is to make sure the team has the clarity, tools, and trust they need to do their best work. Here's how we do it, step by step.

01
Brief
We start by translating stakeholder briefs into clear, actionable creative direction. I facilitate workshops and discussions to ensure every team member understands the "why" behind the work, so we're all aligned before we begin.
02
System
We build and refine the systems — like atomic design and AI-assisted templates — that allow our production to scale. I work with the team to identify pain points and bottlenecks, then we build solutions that eliminate repetitive tasks and free up time for creative thinking.
03
Launch
We coordinate execution across distributed teams using ClickUp and Agile workflows. My focus is on removing blockers, ensuring everyone has what they need and keeping communication clear so we can ship work on time, together.
04
Scale
We measure our results, gather feedback, and reflect on what worked. But scaling isn't just about output — it's about team growth. I make sure we document our wins and lessons, so the team gets smarter and stronger with every cycle.

Throughout this process, I work to ensure the team has clear ownership, the resources they need and visible recognition for their contributions.
