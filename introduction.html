CASE STUDY 01QS-2025

QS APAC Summit

The flagship annual summit — QS's largest single brand and revenue event, run across a distributed creative team.

The brief: deliver creative strategy, sponsorship assets and on-site brand systems for 13+ annual global summits — without the budget or headcount growing at the same rate as the events did.

Built reusable sponsorship & delegate asset systems

Directed strategy across distributed teams

→
Owned stakeholder translation
turning complex sponsor briefs into clear creative direction
